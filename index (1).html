Deskwise: AI-Based Customer Support Ticket System

A support desk that handles a ticket from the moment a customer submits it. Personal details are redacted, then three models predict the ticket's department, priority and sentiment. It joins the queue of one of seven departments, where support agents claim and resolve it.

Access is role-based for customers, support agents, department managers and admins. Tickets that are both high priority and negative also reach the department manager, and admins create departments, assign staff and see live ticket counts. The backend runs on FastAPI and Supabase with a React frontend on Vercel and Render, and the quantized ONNX models are served from Hugging Face.

I trained the department and priority classifiers. I started with a TF-IDF and LinearSVC baseline, then fine-tuned DistilBERT with class-weighted loss, because the support categories overlap heavily and the baseline kept confusing them.

Held-out test data from about 25,000 English tickets. The baseline task used 10 categories, so its numbers are not directly comparable with the 7-department model.

Forest Fire Risk Analysis from Satellite Data

A two-stage framework for fire-prone forest regions in India. A U-Net/LSTM model produces a daily fire probability map, then a Cellular Automata simulation shows how a fire could spread across terrain and land cover.

Inputs are VIIRS fire hotspots, ERA-5 weather, a digital elevation model, land use maps and human settlement data.
